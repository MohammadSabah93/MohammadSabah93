I am a postdoctoral researcher working on **computational geomechanics and injection-induced seismicity**, with a focus on numerical simulation of coupled reservoir–fault processes across quasi-static and dynamic timescales.

My research combines **poromechanics, fault reactivation, rate-and-state friction, dynamic rupture, extended finite element methods (XFEM), and coupled multiphysics modeling** for geothermal energy and other subsurface geo-energy applications.

## Research interests

- Injection-induced and post-injection seismicity
- Reservoir and fault geomechanics
- Coupled hydro-mechanical and thermo-hydro-mechanical modeling
- Rate-and-state friction and fault reactivation
- Dynamic rupture and seismic-wave effects
- XFEM and mixed-dimensional numerical methods
- Adaptive and hybrid implicit–explicit time integration
- Geothermal reservoir stimulation and subsurface energy systems

## Research software

### [XFEM Simulator for Injection-Induced Seismicity](https://github.com/MohammadSabah93/XFEM-Injection-Induced-Seismicity)

MATLAB research framework for coupled poromechanical simulation of injection-induced fault reactivation using XFEM, nonlinear fault contact, rate-and-state friction, inertia, and dynamic rupture.

### [Hybrid IMEX XFEM for Injection-Induced Seismicity](https://github.com/MohammadSabah93/Hybrid-implicit-explicit-XFEM-simulation-of-injection-induced-seismicity)

Research implementation of a hybrid implicit–explicit strategy designed to bridge slow reservoir pressurization and rapid fault acceleration in coupled hydromechanical simulations.

## Current research direction

I am developing numerical frameworks for physically consistent simulation of **injection-induced seismicity**, with emphasis on the transition from slow reservoir loading to fault nucleation and dynamic slip, and on identifying the mechanisms that control seismic response during and after fluid injection.
